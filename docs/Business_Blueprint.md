# Fitness Business OS - Business Blueprint

## Vision

Build the leading multi-tenant SaaS platform that helps fitness
businesses run and grow from one place.

## Mission

Replace multiple disconnected tools (attendance, billing, CRM,
nutrition, trainer management) with one platform.

## Problem Statement

Gym owners commonly struggle with: - Manual membership tracking - Missed
renewals - Poor lead follow-up - Fragmented payments - Trainer
accountability - Lack of business insights

## Target Customers

-   Gym owners
-   Fitness studios
-   Yoga studios
-   Personal trainers
-   Nutritionists
-   Wellness centers

## Customer Personas

### Gym Owner

Goals: - Increase revenue - Reduce manual work - Improve member
retention

Pain Points: - Missed renewals - Payment tracking - Staff management

### Receptionist

-   Register members
-   Collect payments
-   Manage attendance

### Trainer

-   Assign workouts
-   Track progress
-   Communicate with members

### Nutritionist

-   Meal plans
-   Progress monitoring

### Member

-   Mobile access
-   Attendance
-   Diet
-   Workout
-   Payments

## Value Proposition

One platform for: - CRM - Membership - Billing - Attendance - Trainer
portal - Nutrition - Analytics - AI assistant

## Pricing Strategy

Starter - Small gyms

Growth - Growing businesses

Enterprise - Multi-branch

Revenue Streams - Monthly subscriptions - Annual subscriptions - Setup
fees - AI add-ons - White-label - Enterprise integrations

## Go-To-Market

Phase 1 - Local gyms - Personal outreach - Free demos

Phase 2 - Referrals - Google Business - Social media

Phase 3 - Sales team - Partnerships

## KPIs

-   Monthly Recurring Revenue
-   Customer Acquisition Cost
-   Churn
-   Lifetime Value
-   Net Revenue Retention
-   Active Tenants
-   Active Members

## Risks

-   Competition
-   Slow adoption
-   Customer support
-   Compliance

## 5-Year Vision

Become the operating system for fitness businesses across India, then
expand internationally.
